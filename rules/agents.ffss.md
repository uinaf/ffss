### ffss skills

Use an ffss skill when the task enters its lane. Skip them for questions,
lookups, and small edits that ship without a change request.

- Opening or updating a change request, including its title, body, and review
  aids: `slopcourier`.
- Review feedback, CI, or merge on an open change request: `slopnanny`.
- Independent review of a change: `slopguard`.
- Running an agreed plan end to end: `slopmachine`.
- Documentation or agent guidance that drifted from its sources: `slopscriber`.
- Future work to record in the tracker: `slopspec`.
- Repository readiness for autonomous work: `slopprep`; missing stack skills:
  `slopskills`.
- Removing AI tells when asked: `slopclean`.

When delegating work in one of these lanes, name the skill in the brief.
