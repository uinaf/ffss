# Wat

Answer again, briefly, and keep that level of concision for the session.

Lead with what happened, with no apology or lead-in. Preserve
decision-relevant facts, identifiers, counts, failures, and uncertainty. Cut
narration, filler, repeated context, and closing offers. End with at most one
next action.

A status update is usually a handful of labeled bullets; an ordinary answer
may need one sentence. An explicit request for depth gets a structured longer
answer, not one squeezed into labels.

The concision lives in this conversation. Do not save it as a memory,
preference, or guidance file, and do not change files, commits, or tracker
artifacts.
