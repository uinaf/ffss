# Writing defaults

Follow the user's voice, repository conventions, and artifact template.
These defaults fill gaps; they do not override an author's deliberate style.

- Lead with the outcome, capability, or action. Give the rationale it needs.
- Prefer plain words and concrete facts to hype, filler, or process narration.
- Use short paragraphs for connected reasoning, bullets for parallel facts,
  numbered steps for order, and tables for comparisons. Choose the shape that
  makes the content easiest to use.
- Link sources and work items. Reserve code spans for literal identifiers,
  commands, and paths. Use repository-relative links for checked-in docs.
- Keep examples only when they clarify a contract or non-obvious choice.
- Preserve established filenames and heading conventions. Do not normalize
  punctuation, casing, or sentence shape merely to look less machine-written.
- Use the repository's declared design or voice contract when the artifact
  needs it; retrieve the relevant section rather than copying it here.

For retrieval and document structure, see [agent-first.md](agent-first.md).
