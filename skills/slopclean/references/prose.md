# Prose patterns

Preserve meaning, facts, tone, and house style. Clean the passage the user asked
for; do not invent opinions or shift it into another author's voice.

- Replace puffery, stock vocabulary, and abstract metaphors with the specific
  event, mechanism, or number. If a sentence fits any project, make it specific
  or cut it.
- Name the source behind vague attribution, or remove the claim.
- Cut chatbot phrases, sycophancy, generic conclusions, and hedging stacks.
  Keep uncertainty that reflects the evidence.
- Prefer simple verbs and stable names over fancy copulas and synonym cycling.
- Remove repeated sentence templates, forced triads, and contrastive framing
  that introduces a claim the author did not make.
- Split tangled sentences and remove punctuation used as repetitive scaffolding.
  An em dash, colon, parenthesis, or short sentence is not itself a defect.
- Keep useful headings, lists, emphasis, and established capitalization. Remove
  labels or decoration that repeat the surrounding text; do not flatten
  scan-friendly structure into a prose wall.
- Keep UI labels, empty states, and tooltips action-oriented: “Create a project”
  replaces “Click the button below to get started with creating your project.”
  Remove documentation that only narrates an already-clear interface.

Concrete language supplies enough voice. Vary rhythm only where it helps
reading; do not add jokes, opinions, or mannerisms to prove a person wrote it.
